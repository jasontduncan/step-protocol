# STEP evidence

Evidence documents describe observed use of STEP. They do not extend the
protocol and are not conformance certificates.

Every evidence document should state:

- whether the audit is internal or independent;
- the source and snapshot boundary;
- the collection method;
- which claims are supported;
- which claims are not supported;
- confidentiality and reproducibility limits;
- corrections to earlier conclusions.
