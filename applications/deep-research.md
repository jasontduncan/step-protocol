# Deep Research

Deep research often spans sources, tools, context windows, and execution sessions. It becomes fragile when the research brief, coverage, evidence, and unresolved questions exist only in conversational context.

In a STEP WorkNode:

- `PLAN.md` records the research question, scope, subquestions, and completion criteria.
- `STATE.md` records investigated, active, blocked, and remaining work.
- `logs/` preserve sources, tool output, reasoning notes, and claim-level evidence.

Validation checks that conclusions are supported by recorded evidence and that material uncertainty remains visible.

A new human or agent can continue the research without reconstructing the previous session or repeating completed investigation.

STEP specifies this representation in [`PROTOCOL.md`](../PROTOCOL.md).
